About S.R.I Industrial Valves

Built on experience. Driven by dependable flow.

S.R.I Industrial Valves was established in 1994 and has grown from a regional supplier into a trusted industrial valve solution provider in Tamil Nadu.

Located at Karai in the Ranipet District, the company is positioned near an important industrial corridor and supports customers across chemical processing, pharmaceutical, life sciences, water treatment, oil and gas, power and general manufacturing.

The company has in-house lathe, milling and testing machinery for valve servicing, customization and quality assurance. The exact suitability of each service depends on the valve, application and technical requirement.

Our Approach

Practical support for every valve requirement.

We focus on suitable product selection, clear communication, quality-focused sourcing, servicing support and long-term customer relationships.

Our goal is to help customers make informed valve decisions based on service conditions, size, material, pressure and application.
